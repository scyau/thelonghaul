🚐 The Long Haul — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

The Long Haul does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All data you enter into the app remains private to you.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed into iCloud, synced privately via Apple's iCloud service. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Photos

Photos you add to the app (trailer photos, note photos, receipt photos, campsite review photos) are stored locally on your device and backed up to your private iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. Photos are never uploaded to any server we operate.

Location

The app uses location names you enter manually to fetch weather forecasts via Apple WeatherKit. No GPS location data is collected or stored.

Weather Data

Weather forecasts are provided by Apple WeatherKit. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for details on how weather data is handled.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
