Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored privately on your device and synced securely via iCloud if you are signed in. We never collect or share your data.

Will my data survive if I reinstall the app?

Yes — as long as iCloud Backup or iCloud sync is enabled on your device, your data will be restored automatically.

How do I add a campsite that isn't on the map?

The campsite map currently shows BC Parks campsites. Additional campsite support is planned for a future update.
